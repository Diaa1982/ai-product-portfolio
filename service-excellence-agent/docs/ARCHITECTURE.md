# Architecture

The Service Excellence Agent is the governed intelligence and coordination layer between leadership/global service teams and specialist agents/systems.

## Logical model
Leadership / Global Service / Regional Service
→ Service Excellence Agent
→ Sales | Operations | Supply Chain | Finance | Manufacturing | Business Development | Customer Experience | Data
→ Power BI | Tableau | Excel | Salesforce/CRM | ERP | Warehouse | Manufacturing | Supply Chain | Workflow

## Responsibilities
- KPI definition and lineage
- performance analysis and exception detection
- cross-source reconciliation
- root-cause coordination
- corrective-action tracking
- dashboard/report specifications
- executive synthesis
- audit trail

The agent is not the system of record. Official commercial, financial, manufacturing, supply-chain and CRM transactions remain in authoritative systems.
