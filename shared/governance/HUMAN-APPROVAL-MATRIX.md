# Human Approval Matrix

| Activity | AI autonomy | Required human role |
|---|---|---|
| Retrieve approved evidence | Automatic within access rules | Source/data owner governs access |
| Summarize and classify | Automatic with confidence and provenance | Analyst reviews material outputs |
| Calculate using approved rules | Automatic with calculation lineage | Finance/control reviewer validates exceptions |
| Forecast or recommend | Draft/recommend only | Accountable business owner approves use |
| Change policy, budget or authority | Prohibited | Authorized governance body |
| Post or adjust financial transactions | Prohibited | Authorized financial officer/system workflow |
| Approve payment or refund | Prohibited | Delegated approver |
| Issue financial statements | Prohibited | Statutory/accountable authority |
| Close audit or risk findings | Prohibited | Authorized assurance/risk owner |
| Publish a high-impact signal | Human approval required | Strategy/risk authority |
| Onboard production data/model | Human approval required | Data, security, legal and product authorities |
| Deploy to production | Human approval required | Design authority/change authority |

Products must implement the stricter rule when legal, policy, risk or delegated-authority requirements differ.
