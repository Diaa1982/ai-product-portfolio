# Scoring methodology

Coverage is `completed required links ÷ assessed required links × 100`. Maturity uses 1–5: Initial, Managed, Defined, Measured, Continuously improved. Current maturity is reported only when evidence references resolve.

Gap is `max(target − current, 0)`; priority is `criticality × evidenced gap`. Scores 12+ enter W1 Foundation, 6–11 W2 Integrate and 1–5 W3 Optimize. These are configurable portfolio rules, not official benchmarks. Formal maturity remains false until independent validation and sustained evidence exist.
