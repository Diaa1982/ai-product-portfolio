# PFM operating cycle

The 11-stage chain is: sense signals and needs; set fiscal policy and strategy; model scenarios and outlook; formulate budgets; approve and allocate; execute and control; manage treasury/cash/liquidity; administer revenue/receivables; account/consolidate/report; monitor performance/risk/assurance; learn/improve/reform.

Stages are grouped into STEER, MANAGE, ENABLE and ASSURE. This is an architecture working standard, not a statutory sequence. Local laws, budget calendars, delegations, mandates and assurance arrangements require approval.
