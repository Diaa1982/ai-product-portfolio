# Product roadmap

- Foundation: engine, UI, tests, docs and container — complete for synthetic use.
- Pilot preparation: local catalogues, bilingual terms, IAM, exports, UAT and approved representative data.
- Controlled pilot: read-only adapters, design-authority workflow, observability, security/performance tests and training.
- Production decision: L0–L10 evidence, residual-risk acceptance and formal approval.
- Scale: sustained KPI trends, benefits reviews and independently validated maturity improvement.
