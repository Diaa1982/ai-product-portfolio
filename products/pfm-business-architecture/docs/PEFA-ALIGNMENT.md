# PEFA-informed alignment

Capabilities may be tagged to locally validated reference domains: budget reliability, transparency, assets/liabilities, policy-based strategy/budgeting, predictability/control, accounting/reporting and external scrutiny/audit.

Tags are navigation metadata. P13 does not calculate official indicators, apply the PEFA methodology, replace assessors or declare compliance. Diagnostic claims require the applicable official version, approved scope, complete evidence, calibration and competent independent assurance.
