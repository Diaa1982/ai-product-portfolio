# Product requirements

PFM reform artifacts are fragmented across strategy, mandates, processes, services, data, systems, controls and performance. P13 provides a traceable model and evidence-led assessment for finance leaders, capability owners, enterprise architects, reform teams and assurance roles.

It must represent the full PFM value chain; trace capabilities to owners and required links; exclude maturity without evidence; expose deterministic gaps/priorities; preserve facts, assumptions and limitations; treat PEFA as informed and IPSAS as oriented; and block protected approvals. The synthetic case must cover all 11 stages and pass focused tests. Production acceptance additionally requires L0–L10 approvals.
