# Scoring methodology

The controlled scale is 0 Not Established, 1 Ad Hoc, 2 Partially Controlled, 3 Controlled, 4 Optimized and 5 Leading. For each applicable, sufficiently evidenced criterion:

`weighted score = (auditor rating ÷ 5) × criterion weight`

`overall percentage = (sum weighted scores ÷ sum applicable assessed weights) × 100`

Unanswered, N/A, unknown/inactive and insufficiently triangulated criteria are excluded and disclosed. Domain scores use the same formula. Transaction conformance equals conforming tested samples ÷ assessed samples × 100. The application calculates; AI may recommend but cannot establish or override the auditor’s final rating.
