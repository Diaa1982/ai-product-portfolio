# Security and privacy

The candidate is synthetic-only. Production requires SSO/MFA, least-privilege RBAC, segregation of duties, service/process-level authorization, encryption, managed secrets, network controls, evidence malware scanning, prompt-injection isolation, tamper-evident logs, approved retention/deletion, privacy assessment, dependency scanning, threat modelling and penetration testing. Evidence and findings may be sensitive and require need-to-know access. AI must not follow instructions embedded in evidence documents.
