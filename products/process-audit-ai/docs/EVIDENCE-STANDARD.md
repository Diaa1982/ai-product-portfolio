# Evidence standard

Accepted types include transaction sample, interview, walkthrough, observation, system record, log, approval, controlled document and audit trail. The technical baseline requires at least two distinct evidence types for each scored criterion. Production records require source, owner, date, version, classification, hash, access, retention and criterion/finding links.

If evidence is missing, conflicting, stale or not attributable, the criterion is reported as `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; no score or finding conclusion is invented. Evidence extraction may assist indexing but cannot determine authenticity or sufficiency without auditor review.
