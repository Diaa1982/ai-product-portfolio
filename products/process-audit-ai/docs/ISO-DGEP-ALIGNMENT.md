# ISO 9001 and DGEP alignment

The criterion metadata maps process context/ownership, risks, resources/competence, documented information, operational control, performance evaluation, nonconformity/CAPA and continual improvement to relevant ISO 9001 clauses. DGEP alignment covers process governance, execution, performance, integrated services, digital enablement and excellence/improvement evidence.

Mappings support readiness and management assurance only. Accredited certification bodies, authorized government assessors and accountable organizational authorities retain their respective conclusions.
