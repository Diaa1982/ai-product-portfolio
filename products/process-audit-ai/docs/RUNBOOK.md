# Runbook

Monitor availability, latency, failed evidence ingestion, insufficient-evidence rates, scoring exceptions, override frequency, blocked gates, overdue CAPA, repeat findings, authorization denials, audit-write health and configuration drift. On integrity, authorization or unsafe-output concern, stop processing, preserve evidence/logs, notify audit/product/security owners, assess impact, roll back or apply the approved workaround, verify with synthetic cases and record closure. Never bypass evidence or human gates to restore service.
