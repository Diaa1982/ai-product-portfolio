# Product requirements

## Outcome

Digitize enterprise process assurance so divisions can demonstrate that approved processes are executed, controlled, measured, updated and improved using traceable evidence.

## Users

Administrators, EPM administrators, lead auditors, auditors, process owners, action owners, independent verifiers, governance/quality teams and leadership.

## Acceptance

The product shall manage a bilingual versioned criterion library; plan audits; ingest evidence; compare documented and actual execution; test samples; calculate application-owned scores; draft findings/CAPA requirements; preserve AI recommendations and auditor decisions; aggregate division dashboards; and block autonomous assurance decisions. Production requires all checklist approvals.
