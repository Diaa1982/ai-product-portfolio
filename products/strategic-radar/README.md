# Strategic Radar

**Product ID:** P03  
**Domain:** Strategic sensing, risk and opportunity management  
**Baseline maturity:** Product definition

## Public-value proposition

Detect evidence-backed external and internal signals, assess materiality and convert validated signals into governed recommendations.

## Intended users

Strategy, risk, innovation and executive teams.

## Core capabilities

- Approved source registry
- Evidence ingestion and provenance
- Signal scoring and clustering
- Recommendation workflow and approvals

## Human-accountability boundary

No source is trusted automatically; recommendations require validation and cannot create policy or commit resources.

## Minimum product controls

- Approved source and data registry.
- Role-based access and segregation of duties.
- Evidence provenance, calculations and citations.
- Confidence, materiality and exception thresholds.
- Human review and approval states.
- Versioned prompts, models, rules and schemas.
- Audit logging, monitoring, retention and incident response.
- Synthetic data until approved onboarding is completed.
