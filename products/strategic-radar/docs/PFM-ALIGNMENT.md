# Public Finance Management Alignment

Strategic Radar supports the full PFM operating cycle: fiscal strategy and medium-term framework; budget preparation and approval; budget execution and commitments; revenue administration; treasury/cash and debt management; accounting and financial reporting; internal control and audit; performance and fiscal-risk monitoring; evaluation and policy feedback.

Signals are tagged to fiscal studies/policies, budget, treasury, public revenues, central accounts, digital financial services, public debt, financial control, performance reporting, fiscal sustainability, commitments or public expenditure.

## Standards use

- PEFA-style evidence discipline: link intelligence to policy-based fiscal strategy, budget reliability, transparency, asset/liability management, predictability/control, accounting/reporting and external scrutiny dimensions where applicable.
- IPSAS awareness: flag possible accounting/reporting implications but never assert compliance conclusions without an authorized accounting review.
- Medium-term fiscal and expenditure frameworks: distinguish confirmed source facts from scenario assumptions and policy recommendations.
- Fiscal risk: route material revenue, expenditure, debt, guarantee, macroeconomic and entity risks to accountable owners.

The product is not a PEFA assessment, IPSAS compliance engine or fiscal forecast. Those require separate approved methods, datasets and professional judgment.
