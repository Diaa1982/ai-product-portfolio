# Operations Runbook

Monitor health, ingestion freshness, retrieval/OCR failures, source redirects, evidence-verification blocks, version backlog, materiality distribution, false-positive alerts, approval aging, delivery failures and audit integrity.

P1: unauthorized policy/executive delivery, evidence/audit loss, source compromise or sensitive-data exposure—stop delivery/ingestion, preserve evidence and activate security incident command. P2: scoring/config or pipeline degradation—freeze alerts, revert configuration/image and route to analysts. P3: isolated UI/report defect—normal release control.

Source owner manages official status; radar product owner manages scope/value; analysts validate interpretation; PFM subject owners assess fiscal implications; strategy/risk authority approves protected outputs; platform/security/data owners operate controls. Target names, SLA/SLO, RTO/RPO and contacts remain approval fields.
