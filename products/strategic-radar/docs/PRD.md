# Product Requirements Document

## Purpose

Convert verified changes in official government, regulatory and institutional sources into timely, traceable strategic intelligence for policy, fiscal and business decision-makers.

## Method

Framing → horizon scanning and signals → drivers/scenarios → preferred future and backcasting → monitoring indicators. The POC implements governed source intake, signal classification/materiality, dual-audience recommendations, alerts/digests/outlooks and audit traceability.

## Users

Strategy and foresight analysts, PFM policy/budget/treasury/revenue/debt/performance teams, risk and innovation functions, business strategy teams, source owners and approving authorities.

## Requirements

- Only admin-approved industries, staged jurisdictions and approved official domains.
- Ingest/normalize/OCR integration contract; compare source versions; preserve provenance and content hash.
- Block material output when evidence verification fails.
- Separate fact, interpretation and recommendation; state confidence and advisory disclaimer.
- Deterministic materiality route and low-false-positive executive alerts.
- Human approval for protected delivery/actions; no autonomous policy or execution.
- Dashboard, alert feed, weekly digest and PDF briefing interfaces; POC is English-only.

## POC boundary

Industries: Financial Services/Fintech, Logistics & Trade, Healthcare/Healthtech. Jurisdictions: UAE, UK, EU, USA and Singapore. Deep forecasting, full global saturation, multilingual production and direct policy issuance are excluded.
