# Materiality Methodology

The score is the weighted sum of six 0–1 criteria: jurisdictional scope 16%, industry breadth 16%, time sensitivity 18%, evidence strength 20%, novelty 14% and stakeholder relevance 16%. Weights are versioned and must total 100%.

- ≥0.82: auto-alert eligible after evidence verification; protected outputs still require approval.
- 0.55–0.8199: analyst review.
- <0.55: retain as low priority.
- Any null criterion: analyst review, regardless of other scores.

Confidence is reported separately and cannot compensate for weak provenance. Production calibration must measure precision/recall, false-positive executive alerts, inter-rater agreement, threshold sensitivity, timeliness and missed material changes using an approved representative corpus.
