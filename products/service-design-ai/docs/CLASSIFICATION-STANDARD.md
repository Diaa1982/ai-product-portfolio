# Service classification standard

For the configured institutional profile:

| Rule | Classification |
|---|---|
| No explicit request | `PUBLIC_BENEFIT` |
| Explicit request from a government entity | `G2G` |
| Explicit request from a company or financial institution | `G2B` |

Direct individual services are outside this DOF profile and are rejected. Classification needs service-owner validation, legal-mandate evidence and a recorded decision reference. AI may apply the configured rule but may not approve the classification or reinterpret a mandate.
