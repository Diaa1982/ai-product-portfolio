# Gates and governance

| Gate | Decision package | Human authority |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Identification, mandate and classification | Service owner / designated authority |
| G2 | Discovery scope and evidence | Service and evidence owners |
| G3 | Validated AS-IS journey and blueprint | Service/process owners |
| G4 | TO-BE options and prototype evidence | Design/service authority |
| G5 | SLA/KPI, controls, RACI and launch readiness | Authorized launch body |
| G6 | Monitoring, benefits and improve/stop decision | Accountable service authority |

Every gate requires a recorded human decision. AI cannot approve classification, design, cost, SLA, publication, launch, residual risk, improvement or cessation. Segregation of duties, evidence hashes and decision logs are mandatory production controls.
