# Service-design methodology

The controlled lifecycle is: identify need → classify → discover and document AS-IS → design TO-BE → prepare cost/value inputs → launch readiness → operate → improve or stop. Each phase produces controlled drafts and advances only through the applicable human gate.

The product requests missing inputs before or during analysis, keeps facts separate from assumptions, AI inferences and decisions, and supports three-round brainstorming, affinity mapping, option appraisal, Six Thinking Hats, institutional stakeholder profiles, journey mapping, blueprinting, Five Whys, prototype/testing, RACI, risk/control, roadmap and benefits tracking. Methods are facilitated workspaces: they do not fabricate participant evidence or decisions.

The enterprise process architecture is the backbone. APQC may provide contextual mapping only. “Policy 360” is not part of this formal methodology.
