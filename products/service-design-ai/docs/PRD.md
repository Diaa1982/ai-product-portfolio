# Product requirements

## Outcome

Help institutional service teams execute—not merely view—a controlled service-design methodology and prepare professional, evidence-linked artifacts for accountable decisions.

## Users and scope

Service owners, process owners, designers, operations, costing, risk/control and transformation teams. The DOF profile excludes direct services to individuals. It supports identification, classification, design, costing inputs, launch readiness, operations, monitoring and improve/stop review.

## Functional acceptance

The product shall classify services by configured rules; request missing evidence; preserve evidence labels; produce a service card, five-stage journey, six-layer blueprint and end-to-end report; execute facilitated methods; evaluate G1–G6; and deny protected autonomous actions. Outputs remain drafts until human validation.

## Non-functional acceptance

Versioned configuration, deterministic rules, traceable inputs, synthetic-only baseline, least privilege, immutable audit export, accessible bilingual target UX, tested deployment and rollback. Production readiness requires the checklist approvals.
