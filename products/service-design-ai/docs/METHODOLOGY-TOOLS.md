# Methodology tools

- Three-round brainstorming executes framing/evidence, divergence, then evidence-based convergence.
- Affinity mapping clusters attributed observations without changing their evidence labels.
- Option appraisal uses owner-approved criteria and records trade-offs.
- Six Thinking Hats produces white/facts, red/concerns, black/risks, yellow/value, green/options and blue/governance prompts.
- Five Whys asks and evidences each why; it stops rather than inventing a root cause.
- Journey, blueprint, institutional stakeholder profiles, prototype/test, RACI, risk/control, roadmap and benefits tools produce controlled workspaces and reports.

AI facilitates; participants and accountable owners validate findings and decisions.
