# Roadmap

1. Validate institutional methodology, terminology, classification and 54-deliverable catalogue with accountable owners.
2. Add controlled evidence ingestion, persistent registers, transactional decision history and bilingual accessible UX.
3. Integrate enterprise service catalogue, identity/workflow and locally approved ALMAS cost-input interface.
4. Pilot representative G2G, G2B and public-benefit services; independently test methods, security, privacy and resilience.
5. Complete operating model, benefits monitoring, production assurance and formal go-live decision.

No roadmap date or phase implies authorization.
