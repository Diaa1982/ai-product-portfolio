# Evidence and reporting standard

Material statements use exactly one label: `fact`, `assumption`, `ai_inference` or `decision`. Facts require an evidence reference; decisions require actor, authority, time, reason and referenced pack. Evidence records require source owner, version/date, classification, hash and retention rule in production.

Phase, element and end-to-end reports show scope, inputs, evidence map, method execution, results, open questions, assumptions/inferences, options, risks/controls, gate state, approvals and next actions. The configuration controls 54 deliverable identifiers and eight registers. “Ready” is reported only from evidence—not narrative confidence.
