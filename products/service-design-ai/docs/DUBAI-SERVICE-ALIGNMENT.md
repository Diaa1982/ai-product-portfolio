# Dubai service alignment

Dubai Services 360 informs service design and lifecycle structure, IDCXS informs experience considerations, and ALMAS is the expected source/interface for service-cost work. The product adapts these inputs to institutional DOF services; it does not claim compliance, assessment, endorsement, certification or a live ALMAS connection. Local methodology, service, legal, experience and costing owners must validate mappings before pilot or production.

Design principles are digital-first, proactive, integrated services/data, unified and appropriately personalized channels, efficiency, customer-centric institutional experience, service-consultant practice and justified private-sector partnership.
