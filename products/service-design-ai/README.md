# Service Design AI

**Product ID:** P05  
**Domain:** Institutional and digital service lifecycle  
**Baseline maturity:** Product definition

## Public-value proposition

Guide teams from service identification and classification through design, costing, launch, operation, measurement and improvement.

## Intended users

Service owners, designers, process owners and transformation teams.

## Core capabilities

- Interactive requirements elicitation
- Service classification and eligibility
- Service cards, journeys and blueprints
- Maturity assessment and improvement plan

## Human-accountability boundary

The system drafts designs and recommendations; accountable owners approve classifications, commitments, SLAs and launch decisions.

## Minimum product controls

- Approved source and data registry.
- Role-based access and segregation of duties.
- Evidence provenance, calculations and citations.
- Confidence, materiality and exception thresholds.
- Human review and approval states.
- Versioned prompts, models, rules and schemas.
- Audit logging, monitoring, retention and incident response.
- Synthetic data until approved onboarding is completed.

## Release path

Concept → Discovery → Design → MVP → Pilot → Production → Scale.
