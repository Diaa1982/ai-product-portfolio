# PFM Agentic AI

**Product ID:** P01  
**Domain:** End-to-end public finance management cycle  
**Baseline maturity:** Product definition

## Public-value proposition

Coordinate specialized fiscal, budget, treasury, revenue, accounting, reporting, risk, audit and executive-briefing agents through governed workflows.

## Intended users

PFM leadership, budget analysts, treasury, accounting, audit and control functions.

## Core capabilities

- Agent orchestration and task routing
- Evidence-grounded analysis across PFM stages
- Approval queues and exception escalation
- Cross-agent audit trail and executive briefing

## Human-accountability boundary

The system does not authorize appropriations, approve budgets, release payments, post accounting entries or issue official reports.

## Minimum product controls

- Approved source and data registry.
- Role-based access and segregation of duties.
- Evidence provenance, calculations and citations.
- Confidence, materiality and exception thresholds.
- Human review and approval states.
- Versioned prompts, models, rules and schemas.
- Audit logging, monitoring, retention and incident response.
- Synthetic data until approved onboarding is completed.
