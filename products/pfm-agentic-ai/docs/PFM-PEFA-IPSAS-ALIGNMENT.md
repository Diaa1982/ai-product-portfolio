# PFM, PEFA and IPSAS Alignment

## PEFA-style operating discipline

The design supports credible budget execution information, commitment and cash monitoring, internal controls, segregation of duties, traceability, reporting and audit follow-up. It does not claim a PEFA assessment score. A formal assessment must use the current authorized framework, evidence, scope and qualified assessors.

## IPSAS-oriented accounting and reporting

The Accounting & Reporting Agent may identify reconciliation, classification, cut-off, completeness, presentation or disclosure exceptions against an organization-approved accounting policy and cited requirements. It cannot select the entity’s accounting basis, approve journals, certify balances, close a period, approve or publish statements.

## Implementation rule

Maintain a controlled mapping from each use case and data field to the locally applicable PFM rule, accounting policy, owner, evidence and approval. Do not encode assumed laws, regulations, standards versions or delegations as facts.
