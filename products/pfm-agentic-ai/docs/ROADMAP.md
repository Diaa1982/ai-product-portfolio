# Five-Phase Roadmap

1. **Mobilize and govern:** confirm use cases, owners, outcomes, applicable requirements, authority boundaries, RACI and risk appetite.
2. **Design and control:** approve agent cards, decision rights, data dictionary, calculations, templates, transitions, human gates, PEFA/IPSAS mappings and security architecture.
3. **Build and verify:** configure orchestration, source adapters, evidence/audit store, RBAC, monitoring and automated tests; complete independent control/security review.
4. **UAT and limited pilot:** reconcile outputs with authoritative processes, test failure/override/rollback, train users and pilot MVP functions—validation, variance, fiscal-risk, cash/liquidity alerting and reporting.
5. **Controlled scale:** approve go-live evidence, monitor benefits/risks, review agent/config changes, add PFM domains incrementally and retain manual fallback.

Each phase ends with a documented human gate. Production is not implied by technical deployment readiness.
