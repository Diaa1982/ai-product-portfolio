# Partnership Management Copilot

**Product ID:** P07  
**Domain:** Partnership and agreement lifecycle  
**Baseline maturity:** Product definition

## Public-value proposition

Maintain partnership records, extract agreement terms, compare claims with evidence and monitor utilization, obligations and expiry.

## Intended users

Partnership owners, legal coordinators, strategy and executive teams.

## Core capabilities

- Document and register extraction
- Evidence-strength assessment
- Expiry and obligation monitoring
- Utilization and value recommendations

## Human-accountability boundary

The copilot cannot sign, amend, terminate or legally interpret agreements as an authorized legal opinion.

## Minimum product controls

- Approved source and data registry.
- Role-based access and segregation of duties.
- Evidence provenance, calculations and citations.
- Confidence, materiality and exception thresholds.
- Human review and approval states.
- Versioned prompts, models, rules and schemas.
- Audit logging, monitoring, retention and incident response.
- Synthetic data until approved onboarding is completed.
