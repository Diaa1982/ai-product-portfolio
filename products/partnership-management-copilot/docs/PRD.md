# Product requirements

## Outcome

Improve completeness, utilization, accountability and lifecycle oversight of partnerships without transferring legal or approval authority to AI.

## Users and acceptance

Division users request add/modify/delete; partnership owners supply claims/evidence; Strategy Employee 1 reviews; Strategy Employee 2 finally approves; Legal and authorized business owners retain agreement decisions. The product shall extract agreement items as drafts, link evidence, calculate accepted-evidence utilization, flag exceptions, monitor expiry, prepare bilingual reports and preserve change/review history.

The MVP must remain user initiated and contain no custom orchestrator, Power Automate approval, background trigger or autonomous workflow.
