# Agreement extraction standard

Approved sources are signed/current agreements, approved amendments/plans/roles, accepted minutes/evidence and approved KPI definitions. Extraction identifies obligations, benefits, deliverables, milestones, owners/counterparties, dates/recurrence, KPIs/targets, evidence requirements, dependencies, conditional terms and clause citations. Ambiguous dates, missing owners, conflicts and missing citations are exceptions. Every extracted item remains `DRAFT_PENDING_AUTHORIZED_CONFIRMATION`; AI output is not a legal interpretation.
