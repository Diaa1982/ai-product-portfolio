# Governance and decisions

Division users request add/modify/delete. Strategy Employee 1 performs first review; Strategy Employee 2 makes final approval. Requester, reviewer and approver are segregated. A final decision that differs from first review requires justification. AI suggestions, first review, final decision, overrides and register update reference are retained.

Only authorized business/legal authorities may approve/sign/amend/terminate agreements, accept evidence, issue notices or legal opinions, make commitments, close obligations or approve partnership decisions.
