# Notification and escalation standard

For a utilization/evidence request, the owner response is due within three days. If no response is recorded, P07 prepares up to three reminder drafts; after three recorded reminders it prepares a sector-director escalation draft. All delivery remains user initiated in the MVP. The copilot cannot send external messages. Future automation requires approved templates, verified recipients, privacy/legal/security review, minimum necessary disclosure, delivery evidence and failure handling.
