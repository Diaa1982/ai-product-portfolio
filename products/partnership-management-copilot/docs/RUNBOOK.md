# Runbook

Monitor availability, extraction exceptions, missing/weak evidence, utilization calculation errors, overdue obligations, expiry thresholds, review overrides, permission denials, audit-write health and configuration drift. On data, authorization, legal or unsafe-output concern, stop the review, preserve sources/logs, notify Strategy/product/security/legal owners, assess impact, correct or roll back, validate with synthetic cases and record closure. No reminder, escalation or legal notice is sent autonomously.
