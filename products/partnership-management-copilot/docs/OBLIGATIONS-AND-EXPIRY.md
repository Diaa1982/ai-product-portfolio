# Obligations and expiry

Monitor due date/recurrence, owner, counterparty, dependencies, KPI/target, evidence requirement and clause citation. Flag ambiguous dates, missing ownership, conflict, conditional dependency, overdue/blocked, low progress and missing/weak evidence. Expiry bands are more than 90 days, within 90, 60 or 30 days, and expired. Thresholds prepare review packs only; they never renew, amend, close or terminate an agreement.
