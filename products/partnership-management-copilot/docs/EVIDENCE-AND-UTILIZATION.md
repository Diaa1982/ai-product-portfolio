# Evidence and utilization standard

Evidence may be email, PDF, Excel, image, report, Word, link or another approved format, in Arabic or English. Metadata records evidence ID, Partnership_ID/item, source, owner, date, file type, language, hash/link, relevance and submission state. Submission is not acceptance: only authorized reviewers accept, reject or supersede evidence.

Realization statuses are Realized, Partially Realized, Not Realized, Not Yet Due and Insufficient Evidence. Claimed use without accepted, relevant evidence becomes Insufficient Evidence. Utilization percentage equals accepted-evidenced actual quantity/value capped at plan ÷ planned quantity/value × 100. The application calculates; AI may explain and recommend only.
