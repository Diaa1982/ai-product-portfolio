# Partnership lifecycle

Identify/propose → due diligence and design → approve/sign outside the copilot → register → extract/confirm obligations and benefits → plan/evaluate quarterly or configured cycles → collect/review evidence → monitor utilization/KPIs/expiry → improve, recognize, renegotiate or prepare closure/termination decision pack → archive.

Lifecycle statuses are Active/فعّالة, Temporary/مؤقتة, Permanent/دائمة and Completed/تم الانتهاء منها. Status changes require authorized human decisions and full history.
