# Scoring methodology

Actual median and average use valid non-negative samples. Internal samples below five are flagged. Gap is `actual median − benchmark`; gap percent is `gap ÷ benchmark × 100` when eligible.

Confidence combines reliability 45%, match 25%, comparability 20% and sample adequacy 10%. Sample contribution reaches its cap at 30 observations. This is a transparent portfolio rule, not a statistical confidence interval or official benchmark rating; calibration requires expert validation.
