# Product requirements

P17 turns cycle-time observations and cited external references into a transparent, controlled benchmark register. Users are PFM reform, process excellence, finance operations, performance, data and assurance teams.

It must preserve L1–L4 identity; define start/end/unit/scope; distinguish benchmark types; explain exact/nearest/proxy matches; suppress uncited values; calculate median, average, comparability, confidence and eligible gaps; queue no-data cases; and prevent autonomous SLA, staffing or publication decisions.

The synthetic fixture and all focused tests must pass. Production requires licensed sources, approved operational data and L0–L10 evidence.
