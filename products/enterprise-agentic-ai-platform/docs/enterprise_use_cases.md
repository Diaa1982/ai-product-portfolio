# Enterprise use-case patterns

## AI transformation portfolio

Use the platform to collect, assess, prioritize, and govern AI ideas. The strategy, architecture, risk, and value agents jointly produce a decision package and gated roadmap.

## Process and service intelligence

Ingest process maps, service catalogs, workload drivers, KPIs, controls, and customer feedback. Retrieve related evidence, identify bottlenecks and control gaps, and propose automation candidates with measurable productivity outcomes.

## Public finance management

Connect approved budget, revenue, expenditure, cash, performance, risk, policy, and audit sources. Agents can explain variances, detect signals, prepare management briefs, and route high-impact findings for human review. The platform is decision support; statutory decisions remain with accountable officials.

## Corporate operations

Apply the same pattern to procurement, HR, legal, quality, risk, partnerships, internal services, and enterprise architecture. Add domain-specific agents and MCP tools while retaining the common governance lifecycle.

## Production adaptation checklist

1. Replace sample knowledge with approved enterprise repositories.
2. Implement identity, role-based access, secrets management, and network controls.
3. Replace local JSONL logs with enterprise observability and immutable audit storage.
4. Add domain-specific MCP servers with read and write tools separated.
5. Define test datasets, acceptance thresholds, red-team scenarios, and rollback plans.
6. Integrate human approval with the enterprise workflow/task system.
7. Establish model monitoring, cost controls, incident management, and benefits tracking.
