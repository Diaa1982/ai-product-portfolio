# Enterprise AI Operating Principles

AI initiatives should be traceable to strategy, measurable value, accountable ownership, approved data, and proportionate controls. High-impact decisions require human oversight. Retrieval must cite approved sources. Tool execution must be logged, scoped, reversible where practical, and blocked when authorization is missing.

The delivery lifecycle is: discover, assess, prioritize, design, approve, implement, monitor, and improve. Each use case should define an owner, beneficiaries, process scope, data sources, controls, KPIs, baseline, target, cost, risk, and decision gate.
