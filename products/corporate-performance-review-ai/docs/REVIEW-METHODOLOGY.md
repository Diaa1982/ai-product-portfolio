# Performance Review Methodology

Validate → normalize → calculate → compare prior/current/forecast → verify evidence → capture owner explanation → derive clearly labeled inference → propose recommendation/action → quality review → accountable approval → follow-up.

Outputs distinguish facts and calculations from owner explanations and AI-supported inferences. Corrective actions require owner, due date, priority, dependencies, completion measure, evidence and escalation. Target treatments are Retain, Stretch, Recalibrate, Redesign or Rebaseline; proposed targets are always NOT APPROVED until formal authority acts.

Review calendars support monthly, quarterly, semiannual and annual cycles with cut-off, submission, validation, management review, executive approval and action follow-up dates.
