# PFM Alignment

Corporate performance should connect strategic outcomes with fiscal strategy, budget programs, execution, revenue, treasury/cash, debt/fiscal risk, service delivery, accounting/reporting, controls/audit and benefits realization.

KPI evidence must preserve accountable ownership and source-system lineage. PEFA-style performance and control evidence may inform review, while IPSAS-related financial/reporting indicators remain subject to authorized accounting interpretation. The tool cannot certify PEFA/IPSAS compliance or replace budget, treasury, accounting, internal-control or audit authorities.

Useful PFM review perspectives include budget reliability, resource allocation, execution/commitments, revenue performance, cash/debt sustainability, reporting quality, audit findings and corrective-action closure.
