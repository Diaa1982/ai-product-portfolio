# Product Requirements Document

## Outcome

Improve the integrity, timeliness and accountability of corporate performance reviews without altering official KPI records or inventing explanations, targets, causes or approvals.

## Requirements

- Validate uploaded KPI master/results before analysis and preserve source rows.
- Normalize one KPI-period row and maintain stable KPI IDs.
- Calculate status by approved KPI rule or controlled default.
- Compare prior/current/forecast; expose strengths, gaps, risks and dependencies.
- Separate fact, calculation, owner statement, inference and recommendation.
- Track corrective actions with owner, due date, priority, dependencies, evidence and completion measure.
- Produce enterprise/sector/division review packs with source, period, target and data cut-off.
- Require Performance Owner or Executive Approver decisions; proposed targets remain unapproved.

The product drafts analysis. It cannot certify results, change KPI definitions/targets or approve management actions.
