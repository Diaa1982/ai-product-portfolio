# Operations Runbook

Monitor upload failures, invalid/duplicate rows, missing evidence, late submissions, calculation exceptions, specialized-rule queues, review/action aging, source changes, report approvals and audit integrity.

Critical: unauthorized official-result/target change, evidence/audit loss or sensitive-data exposure—freeze affected reviews and activate incident response. High: material calculation/config error—withdraw affected drafts, revert config/release and recalculate. Normal defects follow change control.

Target deployment must name product, KPI/data owners, performance reviewers, executive approvers, platform/security/support roles and approve review calendar, SLA/SLO, RTO/RPO and escalation procedures.
