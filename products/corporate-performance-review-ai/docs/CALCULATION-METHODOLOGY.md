# Calculation Methodology

Higher is actual ÷ target ×100. Lower is target ÷ actual ×100. Exact gives 100 when within tolerance and a closeness score otherwise. Range, Milestone, Binary, zero/negative targets and ratios require an approved specialized rule and are never forced through the default formula.

Where no approved KPI-specific thresholds exist, configurable defaults are Exceeded ≥105%, Achieved 100–<105%, Attention 90–<100% and Off Target <90%. Approved thresholds override defaults and must be versioned.

Variance is actual minus target. Direction must be considered before interpreting favorable/adverse performance. Aggregation and weighted portfolio results require approved formulas and completeness checks. Calculations must remain reproducible and linked to source data.
