# Application rationalization

P15 combines business fit, technical health, criticality and duplicate-group metadata to draft TOLERATE, MODERNIZE, INVESTIGATE_DUPLICATION or INVESTIGATE_RETIREMENT recommendations.

These are investigation routes, not decisions. Security/cloud/data, business fit, migration completeness, commercial/TCO, service continuity, UAT, exit rights and accountable approvals are mandatory before vendor selection, replacement or decommissioning.
