# Product requirements

Enterprise decisions are weakened when strategy, process, service, application, data and technology repositories are disconnected. P15 provides a governed, evidence-backed graph for architects, transformation leaders, business owners, data stewards and technology teams.

It must validate IDs, ownership, lifecycle, evidence and relationships; calculate required traceability; identify architecture debt; analyze transitive impacts; support application rationalization; draft ADRs; and prevent autonomous repository or investment decisions.

Production acceptance requires approved conventions, repository/data migration proof, representative UAT and L0–L10 approvals.
