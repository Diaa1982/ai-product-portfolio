# PFM, PEFA and IPSAS Alignment

PFM Brain supports an integrated information discipline across policy/planning, budget preparation, execution/commitments, revenue, treasury/cash, performance, risk/control and reporting. Master-data control, in-year monitoring, reconciliation, segregation of duties, evidence and audit traceability reflect good PFM operating practice.

## PEFA-style discipline

The product may support analysis related to budget credibility, predictability/control in execution, accounting/reporting and external scrutiny by improving traceability and exception visibility. It does not perform or claim a PEFA assessment, score or certification.

## IPSAS-oriented discipline

Canonical identifiers, period/cut-off, completeness, reconciliation and evidence can support accounting/reporting review. The MVP does not determine the applicable IPSAS requirement, accounting basis or treatment and cannot approve journals, close periods, certify balances or publish statements.

Qualified public-finance, accounting, legal, control and audit owners must approve the organization-specific mapping to current applicable requirements.
