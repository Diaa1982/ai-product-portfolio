# Insight and Reconciliation Standard

Every PFM Brain output separates:

- **Facts:** source identifiers, period, currency, organization/account dimensions and stated action/amount.
- **Calculations:** named formula outputs generated from validated inputs.
- **Risks:** classified exceptions; never hidden because a narrative is favorable.
- **Assumptions:** explicit limitations supplied with the case.
- **Recommendations:** options for accountable human review, not decisions.
- **Evidence:** resolvable references and a SHA-256 calculation digest.

An insight is usable only when master prerequisites, data validation and reconciliations pass. `ANALYSIS_READY_FOR_HUMAN_USE` means the record may be reviewed; it does not mean approved, posted, certified or published. Mismatch, missing evidence, duplicate, invalid reference, SoD conflict, authority exception or missing critical approval produces `BLOCKED`.
