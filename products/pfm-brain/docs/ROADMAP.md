# Roadmap

1. **Govern scope and ownership:** approve use cases, outcomes, RACI, risk tier, human boundaries and applicable requirements.
2. **Establish canonical fiscal data:** approve data dictionary, master/transaction load sequence, owners, lineage, quality, retention and reconciliation.
3. **Operationalize controlled intelligence:** implement source adapters, deterministic calculations, evidence/audit stores, SSO/RBAC/SoD, authority integration and monitoring.
4. **Add governed knowledge and forecasting:** approved retrieval sources, citation/confidence, model/prompt versioning, forecast back-testing, evaluations, human review and safe shutdown.
5. **UAT and limited pilot:** reconcile to official reports, exercise failures/rollback, train users, measure benefits and obtain residual-risk/go-live decisions.
6. **Scale by PFM domain:** expand only after domain-specific control ownership, data quality, independent assurance and post-implementation review.
