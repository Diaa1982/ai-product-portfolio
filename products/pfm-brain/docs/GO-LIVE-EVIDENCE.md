# Go-Live Evidence Register

## Repository evidence

- Versioned synthetic profile, canonical load order, controlled values, protected actions and 20 acceptance themes.
- Deterministic fiscal engine with calculation digest and 15 focused tests.
- API, Fiscal Intelligence Workbench, synthetic reconciled scenario and hardened container baseline.
- Product, architecture, data, calculation, insight/reconciliation, governance, alignment, security, deployment, operations, test, UAT and roadmap documentation.

## Required before production

- Approved sponsor, product/process/data/control/technology owners, business case, benefits and scope.
- Approved organization-specific PFM/calendar/COA/authority/accounting definitions and applicable PEFA/IPSAS mapping.
- Authoritative data contracts, source adapters, lineage, quality thresholds and signed report reconciliation.
- SSO/RBAC/SoD and effective-dated authority integration; persistent tamper-evident evidence/audit records.
- Security/privacy/legal reviews, threat model, penetration test, retention, incident and continuity exercises.
- Forecast/retrieval/model evaluation evidence if those functions are introduced.
- UAT, limited pilot, training/support readiness, residual-risk acceptance and formal go-live authorization.

Until completed, P02 is synthetic-only and not for statutory reporting or operational financial decisions.
