# IPSAS Compliance AI

**Product ID:** P11  
**Domain:** Accounting quality, recognition, measurement and disclosure  
**Baseline maturity:** Product definition

## Public-value proposition

Support evidence-based review of accounting treatments and financial-reporting packages against configured IPSAS requirements.

## Intended users

Public-sector accountants, financial reporting, assurance and audit teams.

## Core capabilities

- Accounting-treatment questionnaire
- Rules and evidence library
- Disclosure and consistency checks
- Exception register and reviewer workflow

## Human-accountability boundary

The product does not determine official accounting policy, post entries or approve financial statements; professional judgement remains accountable.

## Minimum product controls

- Approved source and data registry.
- Role-based access and segregation of duties.
- Evidence provenance, calculations and citations.
- Confidence, materiality and exception thresholds.
- Human review and approval states.
- Versioned prompts, models, rules and schemas.
- Audit logging, monitoring, retention and incident response.
- Synthetic data until approved onboarding is completed.
