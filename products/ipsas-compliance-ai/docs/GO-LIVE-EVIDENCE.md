# Go-Live Evidence Register

## Repository evidence

- Versioned three-review configuration, principle categories, thresholds and protected actions.
- Deterministic journal, reconciliation and disclosure review engine with 15 focused tests.
- API, Accounting Review Workbench, synthetic scenarios and hardened container baseline.
- Product, architecture, methodology, knowledge, controls, governance, data, security, deployment, operations, testing, UAT and roadmap documentation.

## Required before production

- Named sponsor, product/accounting/data/control/technology owners, business case and approved process scope.
- Authorized, current, licensed where applicable and expert-approved policy/requirement knowledge register.
- Representative end-to-end source files, known exceptions and expert ground-truth evaluation set.
- Approved materiality, thresholds, workflow/SoD/authority and accounting-review procedures.
- Secure file/source-system integrations, SSO/RBAC, approval queue and tamper-evident evidence/audit stores.
- At least 90% approved key-field/test-set agreement plus security/privacy/legal, performance and resilience assurance.
- Accounting UAT, controlled pilot, training/support, residual-risk acceptance and formal go-live authorization.

Until those controls are approved, the product is synthetic-only and cannot support statutory or external reporting decisions.
