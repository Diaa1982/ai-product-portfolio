# Control, Risk and Governance Matrix

| Risk | Embedded control | Production accountability/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong framework/policy used | Required approved policy and requirement references | Accounting-policy owner approval and effective-dated knowledge register |
| Unsupported conclusion | Evidence references, confidence and insufficient-information route | Evidence owner and qualified reviewer confirmation |
| Unbalanced/invalid journal | Balance, account, support, approval and cut-off checks | Ledger/COA/workflow contracts and correction evidence |
| SoD violation | Preparer/approver comparison | IAM/workflow enforcement and access review |
| Unreconciled/aged balance | Difference, item evidence and aging checks | Reconciliation owner, escalation and closure evidence |
| Missing/unsupported disclosure | Checklist status, evidence/policy and comparative checks | Financial-reporting owner review and statement support file |
| Material issue bypassed | Reporting-impact and high-severity human gate | Accounting-manager or equivalent decision reference |
| Autonomous accounting action | Protected-action denial | Approved accounting workflow and delegated human authority |
| Outdated rule/model | Versioned config, CI and knowledge standard | Change approval, expert evaluation and rollback |
| Confidential data exposure | Synthetic baseline and security requirements | Classification, minimization, IAM, encryption and privacy/legal review |

Internal audit and external audit independence must be preserved. The system cannot issue an audit opinion, certify a balance or close a finding.
