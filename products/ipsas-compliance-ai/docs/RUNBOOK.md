# Operations Runbook

Monitor health/latency/errors, insufficient-information rate, missing/contradictory evidence, journal/reconciliation/disclosure exception volumes, reporting-impact reviews, protected-action attempts, approval queue age, knowledge/config/model version and audit persistence.

On incident: stop affected intake/review; preserve source/evidence/logs; notify accounting product/process owners, security/privacy and technology operations; prevent use in close/reporting; revert to the approved manual review; assess affected statements/periods; remediate and independently validate; obtain release approval; restore gradually.

Rollback the approved application/configuration/knowledge bundle together. Never post, reverse, close, certify or publish from analytical logs.
