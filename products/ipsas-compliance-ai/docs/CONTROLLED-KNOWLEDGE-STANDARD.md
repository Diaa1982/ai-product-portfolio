# Controlled Knowledge Standard

The MVP configuration names principle categories—accrual and recognition, presentation reliability, financial-statement disclosure, comparative information, financial-data integrity and annual-report quality—but encodes no authoritative IPSAS edition, paragraph or treatment.

Production knowledge entries require: unique ID; title; authoritative source and permitted use; edition/version; effective date; jurisdiction/entity applicability; superseded status; approved accounting owner; exact excerpt/reference; interpretation note clearly separated from source; linked controls/templates/scenarios; review date; and change/approval history.

Retrieval must use only approved active content, return exact citations and version, disclose uncertainty/conflict, and refuse a conclusion when the applicable requirement cannot be established. Legal/licensing review is required before storing standards content.
