# Journal, Reconciliation and Disclosure Controls

## Journal quality

Debit/credit balance within configured tolerance; valid approved account; supporting document; controlled workflow status; separate preparer/approver; entry within reporting period; materiality escalation. The output may recommend investigation but cannot post a correction.

## Reconciliation

Ledger-to-bank/subledger/external balance difference; item-level evidence; age above configured threshold; assigned remediation/escalation. The output cannot certify the balance or close the reconciliation.

## Disclosure checklist

Controlled status, evidence and policy reference for every item; missing/partial gap summary; comparative current/prior information where required. The output is a draft gap checklist and cannot approve or publish a statement.

Default tolerances and aging values are synthetic configuration, not an accounting requirement. Production owners must approve materiality, tolerances, cut-off, workflow statuses and exceptions.
