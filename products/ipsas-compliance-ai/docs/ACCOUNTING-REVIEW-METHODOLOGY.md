# Accounting Review Methodology

1. Confirm entity, reporting period, review type and accountable owner.
2. Validate source, evidence, policy and requirement references.
3. Extract structured fields without changing source records.
4. Apply versioned deterministic checks for the selected review type.
5. Separate facts, calculations, potential exceptions, assumptions and recommendations.
6. Classify evidence strength and return `INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION` for low confidence or missing authoritative references.
7. Route material/reporting-impact findings to accounting-manager or equivalent review.
8. Record the human decision reference, configuration version and audit digest.

The product reports **potential exceptions**, not confirmed non-compliance. Accounting treatment, correction, materiality and formal compliance conclusions require qualified judgement against the applicable current framework and organization-approved policy.
