# Data Model

Common context: case, review type, period, entity, source, evidence, approved policy reference, requirement reference, assumptions, confidence, materiality/reporting impact and human approval reference.

Journal: journal ID, debit/credit totals, entry/period dates, account validity, supporting document, approval status and preparer/approver roles.

Reconciliation: ledger/external balances and item ID, amount, age, evidence, status, owner and escalation date.

Disclosure: checklist item, controlled status, evidence, policy reference, current/prior comparative references and applicability.

Output: review/evidence status, non-authoritative compliance conclusion, facts, calculations, potential exceptions, assumptions, recommendations, evidence, required reviewer and audit record/digest. Production stores must preserve document versions, field-level lineage, review decisions, corrections and retention.
