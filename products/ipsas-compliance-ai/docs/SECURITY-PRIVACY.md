# Security and Privacy

The MVP contains synthetic inputs only. Do not upload live journals, statements, bank reconciliations, audit files, personal data or confidential financial records.

Production minimum: approved classification and privacy/legal basis; data minimization; malware scanning and content isolation for PDF/Excel; SSO/MFA; least privilege and SoD; encryption; secrets management; network segregation; immutable evidence/access/decision logs; retention/legal hold; vulnerability and penetration testing; monitoring; backup/restore; incident and continuity exercises.

For document extraction or generative assistance, add prompt/tool allow-lists, injection and exfiltration defenses, approved knowledge retrieval, citation/grounding enforcement, model/prompt versioning, expert evaluations, human review and safe shutdown.
