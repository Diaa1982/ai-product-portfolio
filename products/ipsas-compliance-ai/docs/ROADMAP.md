# Roadmap

1. **Govern scope:** approve use cases, owners, decision boundaries, materiality and success measures.
2. **Control knowledge and data:** establish approved policy/requirement register, templates, scenarios, evidence and source contracts.
3. **Validate deterministic MVP:** expert ground truth for journal, reconciliation and disclosure cases; measure field and exception agreement.
4. **Add secure document intake:** controlled PDF/Excel extraction, field lineage, evidence comparison and reviewer corrections.
5. **Pilot workflow:** approval queue, SSO/RBAC/SoD, accounting/reporting integration, audit evidence, monitoring and manual fallback.
6. **Scale continuously:** expand accounting areas only after expert validation, change control, post-implementation review and measurable annual-report quality benefits.

SharePoint/email and source-system integration are later phases, not assumed current capabilities.
