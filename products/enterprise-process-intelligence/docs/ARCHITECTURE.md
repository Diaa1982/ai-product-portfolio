# Architecture
`Repository Export/API → Canonical Process Graph → Validation → Traceability/Maturity/Performance Analytics → Human Review → Change Governance → Approved Publication`

Process_ID is the business key and Process_GUID preserves technical identity/duplicate traceability. The graph links parent/child processes to organization/RACI, services, policies/requirements, applications, data, risks, controls, KPIs and activities. Production adapters may connect ARIS, BIC or another approved repository without changing governance semantics.
