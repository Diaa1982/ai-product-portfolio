# Data Model

Context: case, form, FY2026, AED, provider, receiving/settlement bank accounts, responsible employee and source system.

Evidence and controls: evidence references, assumptions, internal/bank references and amounts, duplicate flags, preparer/approver, materiality, expected results and human approval reference.

Calculations: receipt, refund, fraud, settlement, commission type/rate, VAT type/rate, invoice, net transfer, variance and discount.

Output: validation, match status, facts, calculations, exceptions, recommendations, evidence, decision status and audit record/digest. Production adds transaction/value dates, agreement/rule versions, provider master, bank-statement identifiers, invoice/tax evidence, owner/due date, decision/closure history and retention.
