# Go-Live Evidence Register

## Repository evidence

- Versioned three-form, source-report, commission/VAT and protected-action configuration.
- Deterministic settlement/invoice/net and bank-matching engine with 15 focused tests.
- API, reconciliation workbench, synthetic reconciled case and hardened container baseline.
- Product, process, calculation, matching, form, governance, data, security, deployment, operations, testing, UAT and roadmap documentation.

## Required before production

- Sponsor, revenue/treasury/accounting/data/control/technology owners, business case and approved scope.
- Current approved form, agreement, commission, VAT/tax, materiality, tolerance, authority and accounting rules.
- Source/bank/treasury/revenue/accounting data contracts, security, quality, lineage and signed reconciliation.
- SSO/RBAC/SoD, reviewer/exception workflow and tamper-evident evidence/audit stores.
- Representative ground truth, accuracy/false-match assurance, security/privacy/legal/tax review and resilience tests.
- UAT, pilot, training/support, residual-risk acceptance and formal go-live authorization.

Until completed, P12 is dummy/sample-only and cannot drive refunds, tax decisions, ledger adjustments or treasury transfers.
