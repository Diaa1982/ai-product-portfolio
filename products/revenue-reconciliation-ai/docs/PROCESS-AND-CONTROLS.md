# Process and Controls

1. Register form, service provider, period, currency, bank accounts and responsible employee.
2. Validate sources, references, evidence, duplicates and preparer/reviewer separation.
3. Calculate settlement, invoice and net transfer using versioned rules.
4. Match internal/e-Pay and bank records; classify and evidence differences.
5. Reconcile calculations to expected results.
6. Route unmatched, material, negative or invalid cases to human review.
7. Record decision reference and audit digest; authorized treasury processing remains outside the product.

Every exception requires owner, evidence, reason, materiality, due date, reviewer decision and closure evidence in production. The AI cannot close its own exception.
