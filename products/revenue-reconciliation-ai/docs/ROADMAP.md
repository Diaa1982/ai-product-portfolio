# Roadmap

1. Approve scope, owners, form/rule inventory, decision rights and success measures.
2. Approve canonical provider, transaction, bank, commission/VAT, evidence and exception data contracts.
3. Validate deterministic rules against representative e-Pay, settlement, bank and commission samples.
4. Implement secure manual file intake, reviewer queue, audit/evidence store, SSO/RBAC/SoD and dashboards.
5. Pilot owner-approved source/bank/treasury integrations with manual fallback and independent reconciliation.
6. Consider explainable probabilistic matching only after labeled ground truth, accuracy/false-match thresholds and human confirmation are approved.
7. Scale by provider/process after benefits, risk, rule-change and post-implementation review.
