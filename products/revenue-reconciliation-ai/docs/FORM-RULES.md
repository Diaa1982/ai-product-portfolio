# Controlled Form Rules

## Frm.601.2025.01

Captures service provider, bank accounts, received amount, refund amount, fraud transactions, settlement, commission/VAT invoice and net transfer. The responsible employee reviews the evidence; treasury transfer is external.

## Frm.172.2024.02

Allows agreed-percentage commission calculation only. The agreement/rate/effective date must be validated before production.

## Frm.221.2018.01

Records invoices for all service providers and the three configured types. Any tax-refund eligibility decision remains with the authorized human function.

Form identifiers and rules are preserved from the synthetic POC. A controlled form/rule owner must approve current versions and changes.
