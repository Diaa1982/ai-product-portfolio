# Control Catalog

Core controls cover owner/purpose/inventory/audit. Moderate adds human oversight, explainability, evaluation and data governance. High adds impact assessment, privacy/security, independent testing, red team and rollback. Critical adds board risk review, safe shutdown, CEO authorization and continuous monitoring.

Production control records require ID, objective, risk linkage, applicability, owner, frequency, procedure, evidence type/location/hash, effectiveness test, finding, corrective/preventive action ID, due date, exception/treatment justification and approval.

The catalog addresses responsible AI, privacy, fairness, explainability, cyber/prompt/model security, regulatory compliance, evidence retention, interruptibility, vendor accountability, data authorization/quality/lineage/retention and incident response.
