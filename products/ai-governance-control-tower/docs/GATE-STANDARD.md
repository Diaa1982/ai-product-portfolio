# G0–G4 Gate Standard

| Gate | Decision | Minimum authority |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | Intake, purpose, owner and inventory completeness | Product Owner |
| G1 | Value, risk tier, impact and oversight | AI Governance Council; domain risk for High/Critical |
| G2 | Architecture, data, privacy/security, explainability and rollback | Design Authority and relevant control owners |
| G3 | Test evidence, red team, QA and controlled pilot acceptance | Independent Assurance |
| G4 | Production deployment, monitoring, rollback and residual risk | CEO final authorization |

Every gate records decision, reason, actor identity, evidence hashes, configuration version and date. Rejection/return blocks progression. Material scope, model, data, autonomy, vendor or control changes trigger reassessment. Gate approval is never delegated to an agent.
