# ISO/IEC 42001 Alignment

P14 supports—not certifies—an AI management system. Leadership/accountability map to governance roles and decisions; planning maps to portfolio objectives, impacts and risks; support maps to competence, communication and documented information; operation maps to lifecycle gates and controls; performance evaluation maps to monitoring, audit and management review; improvement maps to incidents, corrective action and maturity.

The implementation must be validated against the licensed standard and the organization’s management system. A certification claim requires an accredited independent conformity-assessment process beyond this product.

Complementary references may include ISO 31000, NIST AI RMF, COSO, COBIT, privacy/security standards and applicable AI/legal policies.
