# Governance Board Operating Procedure

## Standard agenda

Portfolio/value dashboard; new G1 classifications; G2 design exceptions; G3 independent assurance; G4 deployment decisions; incidents/threshold breaches; model/vendor changes; overdue controls/CAPA; realized benefits; retirements.

## Decision pack

Executive summary, purpose/value, risk tier and rationale, data/model/agent inventory, impact assessments, architecture, control/evidence status, test/red-team results, human oversight, monitoring/rollback, residual risks, dissent/exceptions and requested decision.

Quorum, voting, delegated authority, conflicts, meeting frequency and named members require organizational approval. Agents may assemble packs and minutes; humans validate evidence and make decisions. Critical/high-risk and unresolved escalations proceed through Governance/Risk review, QA and CEO final approval where required.
