# Operations Runbook

Monitor gate queues/aging, missing/expired controls, model/data/vendor changes, overdue CAPA, incidents, drift/fairness/explainability/performance/security/privacy/audit thresholds, pause/shutdown state, benefits and audit integrity.

Critical: security/privacy/unauthorized financial action/audit loss/critical drift—safe shutdown, preserve evidence, incident command and accountable notifications. High: fairness/explainability/performance/high drift—pause and human review. Low: continue monitoring.

Target deployment must name product, council, design authority, risk/data/model owners, independent assurance, incident commander, platform/support and CEO decision authority; approve SLA/SLO, RTO/RPO, escalation, meeting and release calendars.
