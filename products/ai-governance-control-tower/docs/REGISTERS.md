# Governance Registers

## Use-case register

Purpose, owner, business value, lifecycle/gate, audience, autonomy, data, risk tier, controls, approvals, deployment and benefit measures.

## Model and agent register

Model/agent ID, provider/version, purpose, owner, data/retrieval, prompts/tools, evaluations, limitations, risk tier, dependencies, change history, monitoring and retirement.

## Risk/control/evidence register

Risk and control IDs, impact/likelihood/treatment, applicability/justification, owners, evidence hashes, effectiveness, exceptions and CAPA.

## Incident and monitoring register

Event/severity, affected system/data/decision, thresholds, containment, safe shutdown, notifications, root cause, remediation, recovery authorization and lessons.

## Benefits register

Baseline, target, realized value, quality/service/control effects, cost, owner, reporting period and independent validation.
