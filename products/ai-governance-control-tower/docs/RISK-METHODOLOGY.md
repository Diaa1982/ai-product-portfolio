# AI Risk Methodology

- Low: internal productivity, low autonomy and no sensitive/consequential use.
- Moderate: assisted, human-reviewed or public-facing output.
- High: sensitive data, decision support at scale or autonomy level 3.
- Critical: consequential or external autonomous action, maximum autonomy or prohibited financial action.

Controls accumulate upward from lower tiers. Classification is a governance screen, not a legal conclusion. The accountable risk function may increase a tier based on context; reductions require documented justification and approval.

PFM financial actions remain blocked regardless of tier. High/Critical systems require impact assessment, security/privacy, independent testing, red teaming, rollback, human escalation and continuing monitoring; Critical adds board risk review, safe shutdown and CEO authorization.
