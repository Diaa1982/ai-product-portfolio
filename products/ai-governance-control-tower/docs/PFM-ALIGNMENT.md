# PFM Accountability Alignment

AI governance must preserve appropriation, authorization, segregation of duties, commitment and payment controls, treasury authority, accounting responsibility, auditability and public-finance reporting integrity across the PFM cycle.

Agents may analyze budgets, commitments, revenue, cash, debt, accounts, risks, controls and performance; draft scenarios and recommendations; and monitor exceptions. They cannot approve payments/transfers, post journals, adjust budgets, certify accounts, publish financial statements, accept fiscal risk or replace accountable officers.

PEFA-style control and evidence discipline, IPSAS professional-accounting authority, IMF fiscal-transparency expectations, internal control/audit and legal mandates remain authoritative. P14 records supporting evidence and approvals but is not itself a PEFA or IPSAS compliance determination.
