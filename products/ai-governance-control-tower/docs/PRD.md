# Product Requirements Document

## Purpose

Provide a single governed view of AI use cases, models/agents, risks, controls, approvals, incidents, monitoring and value while preventing unapproved progression or autonomous consequential decisions.

## Users

Board/executive sponsor, AI Governance Council, Design Authority, domain/PFM risk owners, product/data/model owners, security/privacy/legal, independent assurance, audit, platform operations and CEO.

## Requirements

- Register owner, purpose, data, autonomy and evidence before progression.
- Apply proportional Low/Moderate/High/Critical controls.
- Enforce G0–G4 evidence and human decision rights.
- Require QA and independent High/Critical testing.
- Block prohibited financial actions and incomplete gates.
- Maintain use-case/model/control/risk/incident/benefit registers and immutable decisions.
- Monitor thresholds, pause unsafe systems, support rollback/retirement and produce executive decision packs.

Agents may prepare, validate, route and monitor. They cannot accept risk, make legal conclusions, commit resources or authorize production.
