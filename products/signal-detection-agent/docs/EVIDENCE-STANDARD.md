# Evidence Standard

Every candidate must preserve source ID/URL, issuing authority, publication/fetch timestamps, current/prior versions and hashes, raw snapshot path, provenance token, change summary and exact quoted span with page/table/section locator.

Official sources are preferred. Recognized industry associations and licensed data are controlled exceptions only when official lineage is preserved and approved. Blogs, social media, general news, opinion and unverifiable aggregators cannot substantiate material claims.

Weak, delayed, conflicting, partial or low-quality evidence receives an explicit state and human review. Missing or unverifiable lineage blocks the claim. Production must hash and immutably store raw/normalized artifacts and link each derived claim to exact evidence spans.
