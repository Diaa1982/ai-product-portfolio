# PFM Alignment

P04 supports evidence-led monitoring across the PFM cycle: fiscal strategy and medium-term frameworks; budget preparation; execution and commitment control; revenue; cash/treasury; debt and fiscal risk; accounting/reporting; internal control/audit; performance and policy feedback.

Priority detection patterns include material budget variance, utilization pressure, negative balances, liquidity impact, reporting-quality deterioration, repeat audit findings, unimplemented recommendations and systemic risks. Example thresholds such as variance above AED 5 million or utilization above 95% are illustrative only and must be approved per organization, materiality framework and fiscal context before activation.

PEFA-style traceability supports evidence quality and fiscal-control review. IPSAS implications must be routed to an authorized accounting authority; P04 cannot issue an IPSAS compliance conclusion or approve accounting treatment.
