# Detection Methodology

P04 compares current and prior versions/hashes from the same approved source. No change produces no candidate. Materiality combines jurisdictional scope 16%, industry breadth 16%, time sensitivity 18%, evidence strength 20%, novelty 14% and stakeholder relevance 16%.

Composite confidence combines evidence 25%; classification, novelty, corroboration, extraction quality and recommendation coherence at 15% each. Confidence is separate from materiality and cannot replace provenance.

Routes: ≥0.82 priority analyst review; 0.55–0.8199 standard review; <0.55 evidence enhancement; any null or pending/conflict/partial evidence analyst evidence review. No route publishes autonomously. Production calibration requires precision, recall, citation coverage, missed-change rate, conflict accuracy, reviewer agreement and false-positive executive alert measures.
