# Product roadmap

- Foundation: assessment engine, evidence caps, moderation, UI, tests, docs and container — complete for synthetic use.
- Standards foundation: scheme charter, adopted criteria, governance, competence, claim and licensing rules.
- Controlled pilot: trained dual assessors, evidence repository, calibration, appeals simulation and security/performance tests.
- Production decision: independence/accreditation position, L0–L10 evidence, residual risk and formal authority.
- Scale: surveillance, renewal, public register controls and benefits evaluation.
