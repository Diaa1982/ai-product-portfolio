# Evidence standard

Admissible evidence may include interviews, architecture/process reviews, system demonstrations, KPI achievement, governance effectiveness, audited evidence, external comparisons, continuous monitoring, benefits tracking and human-oversight records.

Every item needs an ID, owner, source, date/period, validity, scope, classification, version and independent-verification status. Missing or invalid evidence caps the criterion. Assertions, plans and screenshots without provenance are insufficient for advanced maturity.
