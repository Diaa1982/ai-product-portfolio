# Certification governance

The proposed governance separates strategic authority, technical development, editorial quality, assessment, moderation and conformance oversight. A Conformance Board defines classes, evidence rules, assessor competence, safeguards, surveillance, appeals, suspension and withdrawal.

Bronze–Diamond labels are candidate classes in a working draft. No label may be presented as certification until a legally authorized scheme owner adopts the scheme, establishes independence/accreditation and records a formal decision. P18 always returns `certificate_issued=false`.
