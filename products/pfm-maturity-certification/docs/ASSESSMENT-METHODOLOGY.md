# Assessment methodology

Assessment is evidence-based, repeatable, independently reviewable, outcome-oriented and traceable. Modes are self-assessment, internal validation and independent assessment. Self-assessment cannot enter moderation-ready status.

Scores use levels 1–6. Level 4 requires KPI achievement and governance-effectiveness evidence. Level 5 additionally requires independent verification, at least four sustained quarters, external comparison and audited evidence. Level 6 additionally requires system demonstration, continuous monitoring, benefits tracking, human oversight and at least eight sustained quarters.

Overall maturity is conservatively constrained by mandatory criteria. It remains an evidenced assessment, never a formal rating.
