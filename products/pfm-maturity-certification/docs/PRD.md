# Product requirements

P18 supports repeatable, outcome-oriented PFM maturity assessment across core and enabling capabilities. Users include finance entities, reform programmes, assessors, moderators, scheme owners and assurance teams.

It must maintain versioned domains/criteria; trace every score to valid evidence; cap advanced levels when evidence is missing; enforce assessor-reviewer segregation; show domain/overall results, conformance gaps and improvement plans; manage moderation/appeal/surveillance metadata; and prevent automated certification or assurance opinions.

Production requires an adopted scheme, competent independent assessors and full L0–L10 evidence.
