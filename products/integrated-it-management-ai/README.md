# Integrated IT Management AI

**Product ID:** P16  
**Domain:** IT service, governance, risk, audit, project and operations management  
**Baseline maturity:** Product definition

## Public-value proposition

Unify ITSM, GRC, audit, portfolio/project management and IT operations information into controlled management insights.

## Intended users

CIO office, IT service management, GRC, audit, PMO and operations.

## Core capabilities

- Integrated service-control-project model
- Cross-domain risk and dependency analysis
- Incident, change and problem insights
- Executive technology performance briefing

## Human-accountability boundary

The product cannot approve changes, close incidents, accept technology risk or alter production environments without authorized workflows.

## Minimum product controls

- Approved source and data registry.
- Role-based access and segregation of duties.
- Evidence provenance, calculations and citations.
- Confidence, materiality and exception thresholds.
- Human review and approval states.
- Versioned prompts, models, rules and schemas.
- Audit logging, monitoring, retention and incident response.
- Synthetic data until approved onboarding is completed.
