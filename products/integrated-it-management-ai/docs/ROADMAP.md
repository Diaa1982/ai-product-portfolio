# Roadmap

1. Approve operating scope, owners, canonical definitions, thresholds and benefits baseline.
2. Build governed read-only adapters and event quarantine/reconciliation.
3. Validate calculations and controls with masked representative data.
4. Complete security/privacy/resilience/performance/accessibility assurance.
5. Pilot with limited users, collect UAT and operational evidence, then decide production gate.
6. After authorization, monitor service/value/risk indicators and run scheduled post-implementation reviews.

No phase permits autonomous consequential decisions.
