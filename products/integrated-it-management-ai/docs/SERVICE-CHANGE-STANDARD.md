# Service and Change Standard

Service records require accountable owner, lifecycle state, service commitment source and evidence. Incident indicators use closed, evidenced records: SLA attainment is compliant closed incidents divided by closed incidents; MTTA and MTTR are arithmetic means of provided minute values. Missing cohorts return zero or null and are not estimated.

Change success is successful implemented/closed changes divided by implemented/closed changes. Emergency rate is emergency changes divided by all changes. A calculation never approves a change or closes an incident. Change authority verifies impact, risk, test, rollback, scheduling, segregation of duties and post-implementation review.
