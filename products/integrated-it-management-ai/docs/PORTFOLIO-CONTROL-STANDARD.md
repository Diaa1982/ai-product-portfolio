# Portfolio, Risk and Control Standard

Strategy alignment is the share of portfolio items with explicit strategy references. Benefit evidence coverage is the share with valid referenced evidence. Delivery risk is high at the configured threshold; it creates a review recommendation, not an investment decision.

Control evidence coverage is the share of controls whose references resolve to valid evidence. Effective-control percentage uses owner-assessed effectiveness and must not be interpreted as independent assurance. High residual risks route to risk owners; AI cannot accept them. Control changes, exceptions, closure and residual-risk decisions require recorded authority.
