# Operating Model

The CIO accountable executive owns outcomes and funding. Service, portfolio, risk, control, architecture and data owners remain accountable for their records and decisions. The product team owns code/configuration; platform operations owns availability and recovery; security/privacy owns assurance; internal audit retains independence.

Lifecycle gates are discovery, design, MVP, pilot, production and retirement. Each gate requires scope, evidence, risk, test and approval records. Low confidence, missing evidence, broken links and threshold breaches route to human review. Segregation of duties prevents a requester from self-approving change, risk acceptance, control closure or go-live.
