# Product Requirements

## Outcome

Give CIO, ITSM, PMO, architecture, operations, GRC and audit teams one controlled, evidence-led management view without transferring accountability to AI.

## Users and jobs

- Executives review health, risk, value and exceptions.
- Service and operations owners review SLA, incident and configuration quality.
- Change authorities review evidence-backed change packages.
- Portfolio authorities review alignment, delivery risk and benefits.
- Risk/control owners review residual risk and assurance evidence.

## Functional acceptance

The product validates canonical records, calculates published indicators, links services/work/assets/portfolio/controls, identifies exceptions, produces draft recommendations, creates a deterministic audit digest and blocks autonomous protected actions. Empty or incomplete evidence is not presented as verified fact.

## Exclusions

No autonomous remediation, production deployment, ticket closure, risk acceptance, procurement, payment, contract signature, service commitment, certification claim or go-live approval.
