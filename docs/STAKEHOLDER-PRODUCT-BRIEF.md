# AI Product Portfolio — Stakeholder Deployment Brief

**Purpose:** a concise discussion pack for sponsors, business owners, technology teams, assurance functions and potential delivery partners.

> **Current portfolio position:** all 18 products are tested **Develop-stage technical candidates** using synthetic or sample data. They are not production-authorized products. No output is a financial approval, accounting conclusion, audit finding, PEFA/IPSAS compliance opinion, certification, risk acceptance or go-live decision.

## Portfolio at a glance

| Measure | Current position |
|---|---|
| Products | 18 independent product packages in five groups |
| Technical baseline | Executable services, APIs/UI, tests, containers and product documentation |
| Data boundary | Synthetic/sample only; no approved production data is held in the repository |
| Lifecycle position | Develop complete; Deploy gate not yet approved |
| Common delivery model | Evidence-grounded analysis → governed recommendation → human review/approval |
| Recommended first pilots | P08 AI Use Case Assessor → P14 AI Governance Control Tower → P16 Integrated IT Management AI |

## Suggested stakeholder discussion sequence

1. **Select one product and one bounded pilot outcome.** Do not discuss an enterprise-wide rollout before a representative pilot is approved.
2. **Confirm accountability.** Name the sponsor, owners, reviewers, operations team and final decision authorities.
3. **Confirm authoritative data and integration feasibility.** Identify source systems, owners, classifications, quality constraints and legal permissions.
4. **Agree success measures.** Establish baseline, target, evaluation method, UAT acceptance criteria and expected public/business value.
5. **Agree controls and boundaries.** Confirm excluded decisions, human approvals, audit trail, security/privacy and resilience requirements.
6. **Approve the pilot package.** Agree delivery partner roles, estimate, environment, timeline, evidence plan and Deploy-gate decision date.

## Practical pilot recommendation

Start with **P08 → P14 → P16** as shared enabling products: P08 disciplines use-case selection, P14 governs portfolio risk and evidence, and P16 establishes operational technology visibility. In parallel, select one domain product where an accountable sponsor and representative data are already available. This is a prioritization recommendation, not a production authorization.

## Source and authority

- GitHub remains authoritative for code, tests, configuration, release evidence and versioned technical documentation.
- Notion is the management and stakeholder navigation layer for owners, decisions, stage status and links to approved evidence.
- The live control center displays portfolio status and KPI/evidence records but does not replace formal organizational approvals.
