# Transformation Readiness Assessment — Methodology V1.0

## Purpose
Provide a fast, directional baseline of organizational transformation readiness and a structured entry point into a deeper paid diagnostic.

## Domains
1. Strategy & Execution
2. Governance
3. Performance
4. Processes
5. Services
6. Organization & People
7. Data & Digital
8. AI Readiness

## Instrument
16 questions: two questions per domain.

## Rating scale
1 Ad hoc
2 Emerging
3 Defined
4 Managed
5 Optimized

## Deterministic scoring
Each response is normalized using: `(rating - 1) × 25`.

Each domain is the arithmetic mean of its two questions. Overall score is the arithmetic mean of the eight domain scores. All questions and domains are equally weighted in V1.0.

Overall labels:
- 0–24: Ad hoc
- 25–49: Emerging
- 50–74: Defined
- 75–89: Managed
- 90–100: Optimized

## Governance
The score is deterministic and must not be calculated by an LLM. Published methodology versions should be immutable once client results depend on them.

## Limitation
The result is a directional self-assessment baseline. It is not certification, assurance, audit, benchmarking, or independent verification. A paid diagnostic should validate material findings against evidence.
