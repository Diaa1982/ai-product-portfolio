# Homepage Build Specification

## Primary journey
Understand → Identify Need → Explore Solution → See How It Works → Establish Trust → Assess → Convert

## Navigation
Solutions | Industries | AI Platform | Assessments | Insights | Company | Sign In

Primary CTA: **Start Assessment**

## Sections
1. **Hero** — “Transform Your Organization. Powered by Intelligence.” CTAs: Assess Your Organization, Explore the Platform, Talk to an Expert. Visual connects strategy, objectives, KPIs, services, processes, risks, controls, actions and results.
2. **Proof** — Strategy, Operational Excellence, Services, Performance, Governance & Risk, Enterprise AI. Do not use unsupported client claims or fabricated metrics.
3. **Interactive Entry Point** — Strategy, Performance, Processes, Services, Governance, Organization, Digital, AI. Selection reveals problem, outcome, solution and assessment.
4. **Problem** — “Organizations have data. The challenge is connecting it.” Show fragmented management information.
5. **Transformation Intelligence Platform** — Executive Intelligence → AI Orchestrator → specialist agents → enterprise knowledge model → enterprise data.
6. **Intelligence Cycle** — Connect → Understand → Analyze → Recommend → Act → Learn, with human approval visible.
7. **Solutions** — eight solution families.
8. **AI Agents** — Strategy, Process, Service, Performance, Governance, Workforce, Transformation, Executive Intelligence.
9. **Assessment Funnel** — Transformation Readiness across Strategy & Execution; Governance; Performance; Processes; Services; Organization & People; Data & Digital; AI Readiness.
10. **Example Output** — maturity dashboard, gaps, opportunities, actions.
11. **Engagement Model** — Assess → Design → Transform → Implement → Sustain; Advisory | Platform | Hybrid.
12. **Industries** — Government & Public Sector; Financial Services; Healthcare; Large & Regulated Enterprises.
13. **Methodology** — recognized management disciplines; specialist government modules may support PEFA/IPSAS-aligned analysis where applicable.
14. **Security & Responsible AI** — human oversight, evidence traceability, access control, auditability.
15. **Insights** — thought leadership linked to solutions/assessments.
16. **Final CTA** — Start Free Assessment | Request Platform Demo | Talk to an Expert.
17. **Footer** — Solutions, Platform, Industries, Resources, Company, Legal.

## Responsive
- Desktop: 12-column grid
- Tablet: 8-column grid
- Mobile: 4-column grid
- Complex diagrams become vertical flows on mobile
- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Respect reduced motion

## CMS content types
Pages, Solutions, Industries, Agents, Assessments, Insights, Frameworks, FAQs, People, Navigation, CTAs, Site Settings.
