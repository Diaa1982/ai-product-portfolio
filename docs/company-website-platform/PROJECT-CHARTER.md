# AI Transformation Company Website & Platform — Project Charter

## Objective
Launch a credible company website within days, while establishing the foundation for a broader Transformation Intelligence Platform.

## Positioning
**AI-Powered Enterprise Transformation & Operational Excellence.**

The company combines strategy, operational excellence, service management, performance intelligence, governance, enterprise architecture, organizational excellence and governed AI/agentic solutions.

## Core solution pillars
1. Strategy & Transformation
2. Operational Excellence & Process Intelligence
3. Service Management & Experience
4. Performance & Decision Intelligence
5. Governance, Risk & Controls
6. Enterprise Architecture & Digital Transformation
7. Workforce & Organizational Excellence
8. AI Transformation & Intelligent Automation

Government transformation, PFM, PEFA and IPSAS remain specialist domain capabilities rather than the overall company identity.

## Product proposition
The public website is the acquisition layer for a future Transformation Intelligence Platform.

Operating cycle: **Connect → Assess → Diagnose → Decide → Transform → Monitor → Learn**.

## Initial conversion model
Visitor → Solution discovery → Initial assessment → Results → Demo/consultation → Advisory or platform engagement.

## Delivery phases
### V1 — Acquire & Assess
- Public website and CMS-ready content structure
- Homepage and solution pages
- Platform overview
- Assessment center
- One complete Transformation Readiness Assessment
- Deterministic scoring and results dashboard
- Lead/demo/consultation capture
- Analytics, privacy and security baseline
- English-first with Arabic/RTL-ready architecture

### V1.5 — Diagnose & Recommend
- Identity and organization workspace
- Evidence upload/versioning
- Extraction/retrieval
- Structured AI findings
- Evidence-linked recommendations
- Human review
- Controlled reports

### V2 — Manage & Transform
- Enterprise knowledge model
- Specialist agent framework
- Client workspace
- Action management
- Reassessment/trends
- Enterprise integrations

## Launch priority
Website launch speed has priority over V1.5/V2 implementation. The first production release should be a high-quality commercial site with a credible assessment entry point and clear future-platform positioning.

## Reuse principle
Reuse and expose existing portfolio capabilities wherever possible, including enterprise-agentic-ai-platform, enterprise-process-intelligence, service-design-ai, corporate-performance-review-ai, ai-governance-control-tower, enterprise-architecture-intelligence and ai-use-case-assessor. Avoid rebuilding existing product logic inside the marketing site.
